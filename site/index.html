What The Badger Mauls

badger · six appetites · zero mercy
  • Posts & Reposts — your timeline, minus the evidence. Reposts get un-reposted.
  • Replies — every "well, actually…" you ever posted, gone.
  • Media Tab — that photo set from 2016? Never happened.

How The Hunt Works

badger · chrome-powered · courage included
  1. Open Badger. It launches your real Chrome — no API keys, no developer portal begging.
  2. Sign in to X in the browser. A floating panel waits patiently, then verifies you like a bouncer who respects you.
  3. Pick your prey: operations, delete limit, filters. Username auto-detected via five cunning strategies.
  4. Release the badger. Watch the log as posts vanish. Every deletion is journaled to JSON in Documents/badger-output — an alibi in machine-readable form.

Private By Design, Shameless By Nature

badger · threat model: your own cringe

Everything runs on your machine. Credentials live only in the local Chrome profile. Nothing phones home — there isn't even a home to phone.

Honey badgers don't read terms of service, but you should know: bulk automation can attract rate limits. Badger naps politely for 60 seconds when X says 429.
